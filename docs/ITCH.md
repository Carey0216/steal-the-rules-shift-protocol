# itch.io listing

**Short description:** Don't copy the boss. Rewrite it. Trade both rules in a six-boss showdown, then explore a 48-sector campaign.

**Recommended first play:** Choose **Protocol run** in the lobby. Six bosses, three escalating phases each, one rule slot and an ending. Every transfer gives the boss your previous rule. Clears restore three hull; retries restart the current boss. The longer 48-sector operation saves progress separately.

**Description:** Enter with one blade and one rule slot. Approach an exposed core and press **SHIFT**: take the enemy's rule and give it yours. Steal RUSH to exchange positions, LIFT to ground the machine, or AEGIS for an armor-break window. Cross eight regions, switch solid platforms between world phases, chain anchor swaps and fight guardians after route objectives. Perfect dodges prime a counterstrike. Each clear lights the map, earns stars and pays coins for short-lived gear. Purchases are refundable before entry.

**Controls:** A left · D right · Space jump · J strike · K dodge · Shift exchange · E ability · Esc pause · R retry. Each action has one configurable key. Controller supported. Settings include assist mode, reduced motion, separate audio levels and screen shake.

**Learning:** Five initial lessons, 24 mechanic recordings and hands-on drills. Every lesson has an immediately available **Skip** button. If more clips follow, choose **Skip current** or **Skip all this time**; **Keep watching** resumes the same clip. Skipping the first-time guide begins gameplay directly. Campaign skips apply to this deployment only, and recordings remain available in the Field archive. Menus and guides support English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語 and 한국어.

**v1.1.0 update:** Optional tutorials with current/all skip choices, five-language skip controls, a smaller verified browser download and retry on interrupted loading. Windows and HTML5 builds contain the same gameplay update.
