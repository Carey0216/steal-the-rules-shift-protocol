# itch.io listing

**Short description:** Steal a boss's ability and hand it the one you just gave up. A six-sector action game built for Game Off 2026.

**Description:** Six machines guard the facility. You have a blade, a dodge, and one empty rule slot. Watch the attack, get close when the core opens, then hit **SHIFT**. Cut through a charge with RUSH, launch above a shield with LIFT, or catch a pulse with AEGIS. Whatever you were holding goes back to the boss. Survive the collapsed deck, live conduits and furnace vents to reach the Executor. The sector map saves your progress after each clear and lets you replay completed fights.

**Controls:** A/D or arrows to move · Space to jump · J to strike · K to dodge · Shift or Q to steal · E to use your rule. Controller supported. Press H at the title for longer reaction windows, V to reduce motion, and M to mute.
