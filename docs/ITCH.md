# itch.io listing

**Short description:** Steal a boss's ability and hand it the one you just gave up. A three-fight action game built for Game Off 2026.

**Description:** Three machines guard the facility. You have a blade, a dodge, and one empty rule slot. Watch the attack. Get close when the core opens. Hit **SHIFT**. Cut through a charge with RUSH, launch above a shield with LIFT, or catch a pulse with AEGIS. Whatever you were holding goes back to the boss. Sometimes the safest move is to keep what you already stole.

**Controls:** A/D or arrows to move · Space to jump · J to strike · K to dodge · Shift or Q to steal · E to use your rule. Controller supported. Press H at the title for longer reaction windows, V to reduce motion, and M to mute.
