# STEAL THE RULES: SHIFT PROTOCOL — v1.1.0

Extract the complete ZIP, then open **ShiftProtocol.exe**. Keep the runtime folder next to it. Windows x64; no installation or online account needed. Use Settings or F11 to switch fullscreen.

Choose a language, follow the five short lessons and try their drills. In the lobby choose **Protocol run** for the complete six-boss experience, or **Start/Continue operation** for the saved 48-sector campaign.

| Action | Key | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D | Stick or D-pad |
| Jump | Space | A |
| Strike; hold to repeat | J | X |
| Dodge | K | B |
| Exchange rules | Shift | RB |
| Use your stolen rule | E | Y |
| Pause | Escape | Start |
| Retry current fight | R | Back |
| Confirm briefing | Enter | A |

Watch the marked attack, dodge or jump clear, then approach the open core. **SHIFT takes the boss's rule and gives it your current rule.** The swap preview shows both. RUSH exchanges positions and cuts forward when used; LIFT launches you and grounds the boss; AEGIS opens an armor-break window and reflects a nearby hit when used. Your rule recharges after the next boss attack or another transfer.

Bosses escalate at 67% and 34% health. The bar shows boundaries and the current protocol. The third protocol arms return charges, double shockwaves and expanded pulse columns. Keep watching the warning. A precisely timed dodge primes a single +4-damage strike for two seconds. A blocked strike keeps that reward.

Protocol run carries your rule and repairs up to 3 hull after each victory. Retry restores the current boss checkpoint. The final screen shows run time, transfers and retries. **Leaving Protocol run ends that run.** The longer campaign saves clears, stars, wallet, purchases and settings locally. Campaign gear is not consumed by Protocol run.

Settings offer keyboard remapping, larger text, assist mode, reduced motion, independent master/music/effects levels and shake strength. Assist gives longer reaction and defensive windows. Every action has one configurable keyboard key; assigning an occupied key swaps bindings. Losing focus pauses combat.

Use the question mark for gameplay recordings and drills. Bought campaign gear can be removed for a full refund before entry. Effects begin in combat and expire. Penalty gear only drops in combat.

## MONEY — experimental coin editor

In the lobby, click the coin balance five times within 2.4 seconds. Enter MONEY and confirm, then enter an integer from 0 to 999999999. This changes saved coins only, not sector unlocks or stars.
