## English

- The campaign now runs through **48 sectors in eight regions**. Ride moving ferries, chain strikes on relays, borrow rules for gates, cross scanning beams, hold beacons and dodge pulses to charge capacitors. Existing routes have been retuned and five boss encounters added.
- The lobby, region map, combat HUD and equipment displays have been redesigned. A return arrow leads to the lobby. Popup entrances respect Reduced motion. Audio adds stereo music and distinct navigation, objective and pickup cues.
- Recorded guides play more slowly by default, with pause, half speed, scrubbing and one-second steps. Familiar lessons were recaptured and six new mechanics have their own demonstration.
- Eligible hits now have a **38%** equipment drop chance. The shop states each item's active duration and when it starts. Weakening items remain combat drops.
- The first 24 sector slots stay in their existing order, preserving unlocked sectors, stars and coins.
