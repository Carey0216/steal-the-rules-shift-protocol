## English

- Rebuilt the lobby, shop, guide, settings and transitions in teal and brass. Added original operative artwork, eight illustrated gameplay poses, animated controls and boss windup/recovery feedback.
- Shift now exchanges rules, trades positions and switches world phases. Stealing RUSH swaps positions, LIFT grounds the boss and AEGIS opens an armor-break window. Both rule icons preview the exchange.
- The 48 sectors now include 19 route types, 14 boss encounter sectors and 13 guardian interceptions after route objectives. Every region ends in a boss. Added phase-platform and anchor routes and multi-hit lock devices.
- Perfect dodges prime one +4-damage counterstrike for two seconds. Three new gear effects reward dodge follow-ups, close reflections and exchanges. The index has 23 items; weakening gear remains drop-only.
- Reserved purchases are fully refundable before deployment. Each keyboard action has one key, supports persistent remapping and stays visible in the lower-left HUD. Added separate master/music/effects volume and screen shake.
- Re-recorded all 29 gameplay clips and added new drills. All five READMEs have localized screenshots and instructions. Windows x64 and Web ZIPs are attached. Existing sector numbers and saves are retained.
