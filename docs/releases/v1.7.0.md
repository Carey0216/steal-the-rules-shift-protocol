## English

- Added Protocol run: a complete six-boss route from the lobby, with carried rules, hull repair, checkpoint retries and an ending with total time, transfers and retries. The 48-sector campaign remains available.
- Every boss escalates at 67% and 34% health. Protocol II tightens timing; Protocol III adds return charges, double shockwaves and expanded pulse patterns. Health-bar dividers and phase names expose the escalation.
- Blocked strikes no longer waste perfect-dodge counters. Scene prompts follow remapped controls. Controller A confirms the next encounter; D-pad movement is supported.
- Sanitized damaged save values and preserved the good backup when recovering a broken primary. Added purchase rollback and focus-pause regressions. Compact tutorial buttons now show their numbers.
- Prepared Windows x64 and single-thread Web builds, AI and asset disclosure, submission checklist and validation report. This candidate is prepared locally; publication and itch.io submission require the creator's confirmation.
